AI language-practice app designed, built and shipped solo to the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6752454974) and [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.voqilabs.voqi) — vocabulary management, AI-powered dialogue practice, and real-time speech-based pronunciation analysis with per-word feedback ~2s after speech ends.

Full production stack: streaming pronunciation pipeline (WebSockets, Azure Speech AI, Groq-hosted LLMs, Node.js), Supabase backend, RevenueCat subscriptions, Sentry + PostHog observability.
